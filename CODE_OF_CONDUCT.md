# Code of Conduct

wslog adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks are not tolerated.
